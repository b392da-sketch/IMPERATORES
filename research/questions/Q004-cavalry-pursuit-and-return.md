# Q004 — Cavalry pursuit, return and rallying, Second Punic War

**Status:** prompt given to Ben, 22 November 2026; awaiting answer
**Needed for:** M0 pursuit and rally (specification 2 and 5.6)

**Decision it informs:** the options a cavalry officer has after beating opposing horse (pursue, stop, turn on the infantry, return to the commander), and what makes each more or less likely.

**Already in Notion:** Hasdrubal's redirection at Cannae and Masinissa/Laelius returning at Zama (page 03, subordinate autonomy); Goldsworthy pp. 228–247 on cavalry, pursuit and reserves; Phase XXIX on retreat outcomes depending on the pursuer's options; Xenophon *Hipparchicus* and Arrian on controlled pursuit (prescriptive texts).

**What is needed:**

1. Cases, roughly 220–100 BCE, of victorious cavalry that pursued off the field, stopped, or turned against infantry, with what the sources give as the reason (orders, plunder, terrain, the state of the horses, distance).
2. Cases of routed cavalry rallying and returning.
3. Where the sources' explanations are likely hindsight or praise and blame.

**What ends the inquiry:** 6–10 cases in a table with outcome, stated reason, and source grade. Enough to check that the simulation's options and influences are plausible; no rates or probabilities are expected.
